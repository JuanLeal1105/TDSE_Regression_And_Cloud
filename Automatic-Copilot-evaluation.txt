TA FEEDBACK TO STUDENT
--------------------------------------------------------------
Overall, this is a well-executed submission that demonstrates a solid
grasp of regression fundamentals. Your implementations of MSE, gradient
descent (both non-vectorized and vectorized), and the cost surface are
correct and clearly presented. The SageMaker documentation is
exemplary in its level of detail.

The most important issue to address is the inference bug in Notebook 2:
whenever you train a model on normalized features, you must also
normalize new inputs before prediction. Specifically, for a new data
point x_new, compute x_norm = (x_new - mu) / sigma using the mu and
sigma saved from the training set, then predict with L_hat = x_norm @ w + b.
Applying the trained weights to raw, un-normalized features will always
produce a nonsensical output. Always validate predictions against your
training data range as a sanity check.

For the learning-rate experiment in Notebook 1, consider adding a
multi-curve convergence plot (one curve per α on the same axes). This
is a standard, informative visualization that immediately conveys how
learning rate affects convergence speed and stability.

Finally, remove the unreachable X = build_X(...) line inside the
train_with_gd function body. Such dead code can confuse readers and
masks the fact that the X variable is inherited from outer scope.

These are all fixable issues; the conceptual foundation of your work
is sound.
